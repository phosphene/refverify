# Vladimir Propp (1968), Morphology of the Folktale. University of Texas Press.
# Locus: Chapter II, "The Method and Material", pp. 19-25

The study of the folktale has been conducted largely by descriptive methods. We are in need of a precise morphological description before any historical or comparative investigations can proceed.

Functions of dramatis personae serve as stable, constant elements in a tale, independent of how and by whom they are fulfilled. They constitute the fundamental components of a tale.

The number of functions known to the fairy tale is limited. As will be seen from the subsequent examination of our material, the number of basic functions does not exceed thirty-one.

The sequence of functions is always identical. It must be noted that this necessity applies only to tales of one and the same category, and by no means to all tales.
